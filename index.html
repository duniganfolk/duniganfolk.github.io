Dunigan Folk

I study the role of social connection in living a happy life, and what happens to our sense of connection when we turn to AI, rather than people, for companionship.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Pennsylvania working with Dr. Angela Duckworth and Dr. Lyle Ungar. I received my PhD in Social and Personality Psychology from the University of British Columbia while working under the supervision of Dr. Liz Dunn and Dr. Steve Heine.
